
# Acer Chromebook R11 — CAD Design

**From Laptop to Kiosk: The Acer R11 Transformation**

This laptop was turned into a kiosk due to limited financial resources, as a kiosk is needed for my thesis. Instead of buying a new one, I reused an Acer Chromebook R11 and modified it into a functional touchscreen kiosk to save money.

## About the CAD Design

This folder documents the design and arrangement of the reused laptop components for the kiosk conversion.

The drawings show the LCD touchscreen, the internal components positioned behind it, and the complete kiosk enclosure.

## Components

- **Laptop:** Acer Chromebook R11 (CB5-132T-C9KK)
- **Motherboard:** Quanta ZHR (CYAN)
- **Battery:** AC15A3J
- **Display:** 11.6-inch LCD Touchscreen
- **Speakers:** Internal Stereo Speakers

## Design Notes

The CAD design shows how the dismantled laptop components are arranged at the back of the LCD touchscreen and integrated into a customized kiosk enclosure.

The model is intended for visualization and planning. Component measurements should be verified before fabrication.
