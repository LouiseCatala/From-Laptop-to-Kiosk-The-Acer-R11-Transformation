## 📖 The Story Behind the Project

This laptop was turned into a kiosk due to limited financial resources, as a kiosk is needed for my thesis. Instead of buying a new one, I reused an Acer Chromebook R11 and modified it into a functional touchscreen kiosk to save money.

What started as an ordinary laptop became an opportunity to create something different.

Rather than spending money on a completely new kiosk system, I explored how the existing hardware could be reused and arranged into a custom-built enclosure.

Instead of throwing away or replacing the laptop, I wanted to give it a second life.

**This project is not just about transforming a laptop. It is about finding practical solutions when resources are limited.**

---

## 🌾 Why Repurpose a Laptop?

The idea behind this project started with my thesis, **KAWING**, a platform designed to support farmers.

One of the challenges I considered was accessibility.

Many farmers in the Philippines face financial limitations, which can make owning a smartphone, computer, or other digital device difficult.

Since KAWING is intended to serve farmers, I wanted to provide an alternative way for them to access the platform without necessarily needing to own a personal device.

**That's where the kiosk comes in.**

The kiosk is intended to serve as a shared access point where farmers can interact with KAWING through a touchscreen interface.

However, while trying to address the financial limitations of the platform's intended users, I encountered a similar challenge myself.

**I also had limited financial resources.**

Purchasing a brand-new kiosk or building one entirely from new components would add significant costs to my thesis project.

So instead of purchasing new hardware, I looked at what I already had: an **Acer Chromebook R11**.

The laptop already contained several essential components needed for a kiosk, including a touchscreen display, motherboard, battery, and speakers.

I realized that by dismantling and rearranging these components into a custom enclosure, I could give the laptop a completely different purpose.

This became my solution: **turning an existing laptop into a touchscreen kiosk for KAWING.**

What makes this project meaningful to me is that both the problem and the solution come from the same reality: working with limited resources.

I wanted to make KAWING more accessible to farmers while finding an affordable way to develop the hardware needed for my thesis.

> **The goal was simple: make technology more accessible without making the project unnecessarily expensive.**

## 🎯 About This CAD Visualization

This 3D design was created by **Catala, Orland Louise** using **Autodesk AutoCAD** to help other people visualize and understand how an Acer Chromebook R11 can be transformed into a touchscreen kiosk.

The CAD drawings demonstrate the transformation process, starting from the original laptop, showing its dismantled components, rearranging the internal hardware, and finally presenting the complete kiosk assembly.

The purpose of this visualization is to make the transformation easier to understand, especially for students, developers, and individuals interested in repurposing existing technology.

### Project Objectives

- Visualize the transformation of a laptop into a touchscreen kiosk.
- Illustrate the arrangement of reused internal components.
- Design a custom enclosure for the existing hardware.
- Explore an affordable alternative to purchasing a new kiosk.
- Support the development of a shared access device for KAWING.
- Demonstrate how existing technology can serve a new purpose.
- Share the CAD design as a visual reference for other DIY projects.

## 💡 More Than Just a Hardware Modification

This project is connected to a much bigger goal.

The kiosk is not being developed simply to demonstrate that a laptop can be modified into another device.

It is intended to become an alternative access point for **KAWING**, allowing farmers to interact with the platform through a shared touchscreen device.

The idea is to reduce dependence on personal devices when accessing the system.

By repurposing the Acer Chromebook R11, I was also able to explore several practical benefits:

- **Affordability:** Reducing the need to purchase expensive new hardware.
- **Accessibility:** Providing an alternative device for accessing KAWING.
- **Hardware Reuse:** Extending the useful life of an existing laptop.
- **Practical Learning:** Gaining experience with hardware modification and mechanical design.
- **Resourcefulness:** Finding solutions despite a limited project budget.
- **Sustainability:** Reducing unnecessary electronic waste through reuse.

The project shows that existing hardware can still be valuable when given a different purpose.
